      Finding twins

      Every player's season becomes fourteen per 90 numbers (300+ minutes). Those go through a StandardScaler fitted on 2025/26, with z-scores clipped at 3.5 so a freak number from a short spell can't dominate, and the match percentage is the cosine similarity between two players. Cosine compares the shape of a profile, not its size, which is why a twin can do the same things in the same proportions but less of all of them. By default a player is only compared with their own position group, because a winger's tackles and a centre-back's tackles mean different things.
